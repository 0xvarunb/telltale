# Marine casualty report sources: reuse terms survey

Checked 30 Oct 2026. Rule: a source goes into the tool only when its reuse terms are confirmed from
the publisher's own page or report. Search-engine summaries are not enough: one said TAIC "cannot
grant permission", but TAIC's own copyright page licenses its material under CC BY 4.0.

"Format" = whether one recent report PDF had a normal text layer (digital) or not (scanned). All
reports opened in this survey were digital text.

## Recommended order

1. **MAIB** (stage 1).
2. **ATSB** (next): CC BY 4.0, English, digital.
3. **NTSB**: public domain, English. Use its CAROL export rather than the report listing page.
4. **TAIC**: CC BY 4.0, but fewer cases relevant to deep-sea merchant ships, so lower priority.
5. **Singapore TSIB**: public reports, cited and linked as above.
6. **Ask permission**, in order of relevance to officers on deep-sea merchant ships: Marshall
   Islands, Malta, Bahamas (confirmation only), Denmark, Norway, Germany, Netherlands,
   Ireland. Liberia, Panama, Hong Kong and Japan after that.

Realistic target for the public link (end of stage 3): MAIB, ATSB and NTSB, all with confirmed terms.
Sources that grant permission join after, each with its own build post.
